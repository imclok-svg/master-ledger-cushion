2. The Functional Split as Sovereignty

He speaks with the smooth, measured resonance of an actor whose livelihood is guaranteed by a university budget committee. Your ‘AI Automation Cushion’ is nothing more than an intellectualized coping mechanism for your withdrawal from the institutional matrix.”
