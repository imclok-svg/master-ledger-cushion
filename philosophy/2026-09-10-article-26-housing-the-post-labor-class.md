### 2.1 The Hikikomori / Otaku Trajectory (The Closed Feedback Loop)

The individual secludes themselves spatially to consume low-density digital tokens—such as infinite social media scrolls, repetitive algorithmic entertainment streams, or closed gaming networks. 

Because this state involves recursively ingesting pre-processed, synthetic media, the node does not generate new value. It mirrors the exact technical process of **Model Collapse** within its own cognitive architecture.
