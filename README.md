# Master Ledger Cushion: An Open Archive on Work, Value, and Distribution After Automation

An open archive of essays and working proposals about human dignity, income, and housing when automation separates earning a living from doing work. Its central idea is the Appreciator: when machines can produce nearly unlimited output, the scarce thing is human attention, judgment, and the capacity to recognize what is worth keeping. The archive asks how an economy could recognize and support that capacity, and builds toward a set of proposals it calls the AI Automation Cushion.

## How it is made

The archive is written in ongoing dialogue with AI systems. The author, Dr. Ian Lok (樂美志), sets the argument and the framework, corrects the drafts, decides what stays and what is cut, and takes responsibility for what is published under his name. Each essay opens with an Editor's Note describing this process in more detail.

## Proposals open to revision

The economic and infrastructure ideas in this archive, including the targeted compute tax (LICT), the voxel housing concept (AVA), and the labor-split ratios used to describe how the archive is written, are proposals. They have not been tested, and several have been revised or reframed as the reasoning developed. They are published to be examined, critiqued, and changed, not as finished policy.
